You are the new operator for the One Way Express. The One Way Express is a cargo train that never stops, utilizing a cannon to unload cargo at Depot Zones. The most expensive and valuable cargo is placed aboard the One Way Express due to the nonstop characteristic of the train. That doesn't mean that there aren't evildoers out there trying to get a piece of that pie. Fight off different gangs consisting of train robbers and vultures, and safely unload your cargo. You will come across three main gangs during your efforts on the One Way Express:
1. The Blind Bandits
2. The Vultures
3. Dynamite Frenzy
After getting past Dynamite Frenzy, the dispatcher notices that Trigger-Happy Tyson got a *little* too trigger happy, and blew up the bridge connecting two Depot Zones together. Looks like this'll be the end of the One Way Express--but not before delivering the last of its cargo. The only way to unload everything? It's to deliver the train itself... Drive off the bridge and deliver that damn train, Operator! Thank you for riding the One Way Express.
