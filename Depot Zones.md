After players reach a certain point in the level, they will reach a Depot Zone. This is where players not only drop off their cargo, but refill on Repair Boards as well.

## Dropping Off Cargo
Players will use the cannon to shoot into Depot Zones. This will load cargo into those zones and increase the amount of points(TBD) the player gets. You can shoot multiple times into each zone--with each shot increasing the amount of points you get. The players will have to balance between shooting these and defeating enemies/obstacles.

## Repair Boards
As you pass by the Depot Zones, players will get (x) amount of Repair Boards back. There are used in [[Repairs]].
